App icon design for a voice-note-taking app called "Beaver". Flat, minimal, modern app-icon style similar to Otter.ai's icon: a simple, friendly beaver face/head illustrated as a clean geometric mark, centered and cropped tight so the face fills most of the frame. Solid bright blue background (#007AFF), no gradients, no drop shadows, no textures. The beaver face is rendered in a single flat white (or very light) fill with minimal internal linework — round face, small rounded ears, two simple dot or oval eyes, a soft snout, and two small front teeth peeking out for character. No fur texture, no photorealism, no 3D shading — pure flat vector/icon illustration style, like a modern SaaS app icon (think Slack, Notion, Otter.ai). Square canvas, 1024x1024px, generous padding around the mascot so it isn't cropped by iOS's rounded-corner mask. No text, no wordmark, no watermark.
